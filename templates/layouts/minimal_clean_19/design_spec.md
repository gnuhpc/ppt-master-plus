# Minimal Clean 19 — Design Specification

## I. Template Overview
- **Use Cases**: Tech product launches, digital strategy summaries, internet business proposals.
- **Design Tone**: Tech-oriented, futuristic, deep slate/blue-gray theme.
- **Theme Mode**: Dark mode (deep slate gray background).

## II. Color Scheme
- **Deep Slate**: `#363F4A` (Primary background tone)
- **Tech Blue**: `#0070C0` (High contrast neon highlights)
- **Light Gray**: `#F2F2F2` (Main typography)

## III. Signature Design Elements
- **Network Nodes**: Fine geometric lines and dots suggesting digital connectivity.
- **Glowing Accents**: Neon blue linear gradient vectors structuring pages.
