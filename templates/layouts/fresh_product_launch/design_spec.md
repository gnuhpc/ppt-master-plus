# Fresh Product Launch — Design Specification

## I. Template Overview
- **Use Cases**: Product launch announcements, creative project proposals, design briefings.
- **Design Tone**: Fresh, natural, minimalist, clean gray background with leaf accents.
- **Theme Mode**: Light mode (soft warm gray/green canvas background `#F2F2F2`).

## II. Color Scheme
- **Forest Slate**: `#3E4241` / `#000000` (Main text)
- **Leaves Green**: (Photographic leaf elements)
- **Warm Light Gray**: `#F2F2F2` (Background)

## III. Signature Design Elements
- **Leaf Accents**: High-quality natural green leaf textures at edges.
- **Vertical Bullet Labels**: Small rotated texts on cover & inner pages.
