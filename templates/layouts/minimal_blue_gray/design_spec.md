# Minimal Blue Gray — Design Specification

## I. Template Overview
- **Use Cases**: General business reports, plan reviews, minimalist slide shows.
- **Design Tone**: Ultra-minimalist, clean, modern, slate gray & blue scheme.
- **Theme Mode**: Light mode (pure white canvas background).

## II. Color Scheme
- **Slate Gray**: `#44546A` (Primary typography and line anchors)
- **Background**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white background)
- **Light Gray Decor**: `#D9D9D9` (Background accent squares)

## III. Signature Design Elements
- **Minimal Grid**: Header contains three gray offset rounded squares at top-left.
- **Classic 宋体 (Serif)**: Clean large serif Chinese characters on cover & ending.
