# Business Proposal Minimal — Design Specification

## I. Template Overview
- **Use Cases**: Pitch decks, corporate proposals, general business reports.
- **Design Tone**: High-end corporate, traditional serif feel (思源宋体/Source Han Serif SC).
- **Theme Mode**: Light mode (warm off-white background `#F8F8F8`).

## II. Color Scheme
- **Slate Navy**: `#1A1F29` (Primary background blocks and titles)
- **Deep Bronze**: `#AF935C` (Accent highlights and lines)
- **Soft Light Off-White**: `#F8F8F8` (Canvas background)

## III. Signature Design Elements
- **Right Side Split Image**: Large vertical image strip aligned on the right.
- **Serif Typography**: Heavy usage of elegant serif fonts for key figures and headings.
