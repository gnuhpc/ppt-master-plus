# Creative Watercolor Multihued — Design Specification

## I. Template Overview
- **Use Cases**: Creative brainstorms, artistic projects, informal work briefings, education presentations.
- **Design Tone**: Fun, artistic, hand-drawn sketch look with organic watercolor blobs.
- **Theme Mode**: Light mode (pure white canvas `#FFFFFF`).

## II. Color Scheme
- **Pastel Red**: `#FF3E3E`
- **Pastel Yellow**: `#F7B63E`
- **Pastel Blue**: `#76AADB`
- **Pastel Green**: `#70AD47`
- **Charcoal**: `#2E2E2E` (Main text)

## III. Signature Design Elements
- **Sketch Accents**: Hand-drawn borders, dotted connection lines, organic scribble outlines.
- **Vibrant Blobs**: Faint watercolor ovals in warm pastel tones acting as text backgrounds and bullet indicators.
