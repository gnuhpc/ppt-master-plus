# Visual style: dark-serif

Near-black canvas, CJK serif as the primary voice, understated luxury. Ink-dark backgrounds with warm off-white text and one restrained metallic accent. For high-stakes business plans, formal pitches, legal / finance, investment memoranda, premium academic.

---

## 1. Shape & decoration

- Shape language: sharp rectangles and hairline rules (`rx="0"`). Thin divider lines (0.5–1 px) as the only structural decoration.
- Decoration: none beyond hairlines and folios. The ink-dark field is the aesthetic; ornament would cheapen it.
- Whitespace: contained but deliberate — tighter than editorial, looser than brutalist; every element has clear breathing room without the page feeling empty.
- Layout is column-structured: a narrow folio / label column paired with a wide content column. Cover pages may use full-bleed dark with centered or left-aligned type.

## 2. Typography character

- Primary: a CJK serif (宋体 / Source Han Serif / 明朝体 character) — the traditional bookish authority voice. Headings at regular-to-medium weight; subheadings at light.
- Latin: a transitional or old-style serif that matches the classical register (a geometric sans feels cold in this context).
- On dark backgrounds, type is off-white or warm cream — never pure `#FFFFFF`, which reads as harsh under projection.
- Weight restraint: avoid heavy/black weights except for single-word callouts or numerals used as graphic elements.

> Families are chosen at confirmation `g`; this style asks for **CJK serif + transitional Latin serif** character — not a specific font.

## 3. Using the deck's colors

- The dark field (`#1A1F29` zone or equivalent near-black) dominates cover and chapter pages; body slides may lift to a dark-gray mid-tone.
- One metallic accent (restrained gold, aged silver, or dusty brass) for folios, hairlines, and key numerical callouts only — used in <5 % of total canvas area.
- Data visualization retains the dark canvas; chart lines / bars in off-white and the one accent hue.

> HEX values come from confirmation `e`; this style governs the **near-black dominant + single metallic accent** discipline — it names no colors.

## 4. Texture / elevation

- Near-flat. Optional very-subtle paper grain or cloth texture (≤3 % opacity SVG filter) on the dark field to add depth without breaking the ink impression.
- No glows, no neon highlights, no translucent cards — those belong to `dark-tech` or `glassmorphism`. This style is matte and print-referencing.
- Elevation: none. All layers read as ink on a single plane.

## 5. Paired image-rendering

`editorial` — formal editorial illustration or fine-art photography on dark ground. Avoid vibrant-color illustrations; the rendering must maintain the ink-dark register.
