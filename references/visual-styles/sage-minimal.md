# Visual style: sage-minimal

Muted-earth breathing room. Sage, moss, and slate tones; ultra-light CJK typography; vast whitespace that feels like fresh air. For product launches, lifestyle brands, wellness, sustainable / ESG, clean-tech, premium consumer.

---

## 1. Shape & decoration

- Shape language: soft rectangles with very slight rounding (`rx` 4–8), thin single-weight lines as delicate accents. No heavy blocks or hard borders.
- Decoration: minimal botanical or geometric micro-motifs as texture (a thin leaf silhouette, a soft circle); all decorative elements are low-opacity (10–20 %) and never compete with content.
- Whitespace: extremely generous — padding inside containers 2–3× the content height; margins wider than most styles. Pages feel spacious and unhurried.
- Layout tends to asymmetric — image or color swatch on one third, content occupying the remaining two thirds with its own breathing room.

## 2. Typography character

- CJK: ultra-light or thin weight (ExtraLight / W1–W2) as the headline voice; regular weight for body. The thinness reads as refinement, not weakness.
- Latin companion: a humanist sans at light–regular weight; avoid industrial grotesques.
- Generous line spacing (1.6–1.8×) and loose letter-spacing reinforce the airy feeling.
- Headline sizes are moderate (not dramatic); this style avoids visual shouting.

> Families are chosen at confirmation `g`; this style asks for **ultra-light CJK + humanist sans** character — not a specific font.

## 3. Using the deck's colors

- Palette stays within a single muted hue family — sage green, dusty teal, warm slate, or muted terracotta — plus near-white and one charcoal-gray text tone.
- Color is used as wash, not block: large low-saturation tint fills rather than solid color panels; the accent color touches labels, thin rules, and one repeated motif only.
- No high-chroma colors; even the "accent" reads as muted from arm's length.

> HEX values come from confirmation `e`; this style governs the **single muted hue + near-white** restraint — it names no colors.

## 4. Texture / elevation

- Flat with optional very-faint paper grain (SVG `feTurbulence` at ≤4 % opacity) to add warmth — use only on cover/chapter backgrounds.
- No shadows, no elevated cards. Depth comes from whitespace and weight contrast, not material depth cues.

## 5. Paired image-rendering

`nature` — organic, soft-light botanical or landscape photography fits the earthy palette. For illustrations, `flat` at low saturation aligned to the muted hue family.
